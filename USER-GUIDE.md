## 🎮 How to Use Math Bru

### First Time Setup

1. **Choose Your Language**
   - Click the language button (top right)
   - Select: English, Afrikaans, Zulu, Xhosa, Spanish, or French

2. **Start Learning**
   - Tap "Start Learning"
   - Choose a math topic
   - Select difficulty (Easy, Medium, or Hard)
   - Start your quiz!

### Understanding the Game

#### 🪙 Coins
- Earn coins for correct answers
- Easy questions: 10 coins
- Medium questions: 20 coins
- Hard questions: 30 coins
- Use coins to buy power-ups!

#### ⭐ XP & Levels
- Gain experience (XP) for each correct answer
- Level up every 100 XP
- Higher levels = bragging rights!

#### 🔥 Streaks
- Answer correctly in a row to build a streak
- 3+ streak: 1.5x bonus
- 5+ streak: 2x bonus
- 10+ streak: 3x bonus!

#### 🎁 Power-Ups
Buy power-ups with your coins:
- **💡 Hint (50 coins)**: Get a helpful clue
- **🎯 50/50 (100 coins)**: Remove 2 wrong answers

#### 🏆 Achievements
Unlock 10 achievements:
- 🌟 First Steps: Complete your first quiz
- 🎯 Quick Learner: Answer 5 questions correctly
- 🔥 Streak Master: Get a 10-answer streak
- 🧮 Mathematics Master: Complete 50 quizzes
- ...and 6 more to discover!

## 💡 Tips for Students

### Study Smart:
1. **Practice daily** - Even 10 minutes helps!
2. **Start easy** - Build confidence first
3. **Use hints wisely** - Learn, don't just guess
4. **Build streaks** - Focus and get that 3x bonus!
5. **Try all topics** - Discover what you enjoy
6. **Unlock achievements** - Make it a game!

### Best Practice Schedule:
- **Monday-Friday**: 15 minutes per day
- **Weekend**: Try a harder difficulty level
- **Before tests**: Focus on weak topics
